Our Journey

The Story of IP Software

From a small startup to a trusted technology partner — here's how we've grown over the years.

2018

The Beginning

IP Software Technologies was founded with a vision to deliver premium software solutions. Starting with a team of 3 passionate developers, we took on our first web development project and delivered it with excellence.

2019

First Major Milestone

We expanded our team to 10 members and secured our first enterprise ERP project. This marked our entry into complex business solutions and established our reputation in the industry.

2020

Going Mobile

We diversified into mobile app development with Flutter, launching our first cross-platform mobile application. Despite global challenges, we grew by 40% and onboarded 15+ new clients.

2021

Enterprise Expansion

Our team grew to 20+ professionals. We launched multiple CRM and HR management systems, becoming a trusted partner for enterprise digital transformation across healthcare, education, and retail sectors.

2022

50+ Projects Milestone

We crossed the milestone of 50 completed projects. Our e-commerce solutions helped clients increase their online sales by over 200%. We also introduced our 24/7 support initiative.

2023

Global Recognition

We expanded our client base internationally, serving businesses in the UK, USA, and Middle East. Our team reached 25+ members, and we were recognized as a top software development company.

2024 - Present

Leading the Future

With 150+ projects delivered, 50+ happy clients, and a team of 30+ experts, we continue to push boundaries. We're now exploring AI/ML integration and cloud-native solutions to serve the next generation of digital businesses.
